Good questions

The things people usually ask.

Does solar actually work in Tasmania?
Yes. Summer is excellent, and winter is lower but still useful. The key is sizing for our seasons, which is why I always look at your winter usage, not just the yearly average.
How does off-grid cope with a week of grey weather?
That's what battery capacity and a backup generator are for. I size storage to carry you through typical stretches and set the generator to kick in automatically if the battery gets low, so you're never left in the dark.
Is a battery worth it for my place?
It depends on when you use power. If most of your use is evenings, a battery usually makes sense, especially with the current federal battery discount. I'll show you the numbers both ways.
Can you fix or upgrade a system someone else installed?
Yes, a fair bit of my work is fault-finding and upgrading older systems. Old batteries, failing inverters and systems that never quite kept up are all common jobs.
How far ahead are you booked?
Usually a few weeks, sometimes more in the busy months. Get in touch early and I'll lock in a site visit and give you a realistic install window.
Do you do commercial and remote sites?
Yes. My instrumentation background means remote monitoring, control systems and commercial power are all in scope.
